# NatLib social media guidance (Aotearoa, Again)

Operational summary of National Library / Alexander Turnbull Library guidance for this project (email reply, July 2026).

## Upload-eligible subset (Meta / Instagram OK)

For the majority of collection items, NatLib asks that we **share links** rather than upload image files to social platforms (including Meta and Instagram).

Images that **may** be saved, modified/remixed, and uploaded to social media:

- NatLib: [ATL free download photos](http://natlib.govt.nz/photos?il[atl_free_download]=true)
- DigitalNZ equivalent: ATL + **Use commercially**  
  https://digitalnz.org/records?text=&i[content_partner]=Alexander%20Turnbull%20Library&i[usage]=Use%20commercially&page=1

This app **narrows harvest** to DigitalNZ ATL images with both **Modify** and **Use commercially**, so every pipeline candidate is Meta-upload-eligible. That shrinks the candidate pool versus Modify-only harvest.

Out of copyright / appropriate CC material may also be composited and uploaded with caption acknowledgement; we rely on the Use commercially filter as the practical proxy for the free-download set.

## Caption requirements

When uploading transformed files to Instagram, acknowledge:

- Source image (partner, creator, rights, record URL, via DigitalNZ)
- Modifications made (AI colourisation; interpretive colours; branded share treatment)

## People and tikanga

For images including people:

- Overlays must respect the dignity and mana of those visible and their living descendants
- Avoid obscuring, blurring, or cropping through people, especially faces
- Seek community advice if unsure about tikanga

Admin review is the gate for dignity concerns. Instagram uses the same branded share image as the website/email (diagonal blend + attribution bar); pause approval if the blend cuts through faces or otherwise feels inappropriate.

## Other platforms

Non-Meta sites are case-by-case. If a platform claims ownership of uploaded content, avoid material where third-party rights may apply (including subjects' rights regarding commercial use of likeness).

## Contacting NatLib

No need to contact them for every reuse. Reach out if unsure about third-party rights or reuse guidelines.

## Takedown

Library process: https://natlib.govt.nz/about-us/strategy-and-policy/takedown-or-request-a-review-process

Generally the Library only contacts third parties if directed by the person who requested takedown. Local console steps remain in [deploy.md](deploy.md).
